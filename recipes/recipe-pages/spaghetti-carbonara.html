Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

← Home Page

A rich and silky Roman classic made with just a handful of ingredients — crisp guanciale, salty Pecorino Romano, and eggs whisked into a glossy sauce that clings to every strand of pasta. No cream needed here; the magic comes from tossing hot pasta with the egg mixture off the heat, creating a velvety texture through emulsion alone. Finished with cracked black pepper, it's simple, comforting, and endlessly satisfying.

Ingrediant sizing is intended for 4 servings:
  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 8 ounces guanciale (or pancetta), diced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup Pecorino Romano cheese, grated
  • 0.5 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 teaspoons freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt, to taste for pasta water

Steps:

  1. Boil the pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add 1 pound spaghetti and cook until al dente, about 10 minutes 10:00. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Crisp the guanciale: While the pasta cooks, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add 8 ounces guanciale, diced and cook until golden and crispy, about 6 minutes 06:00.
  3. Whisk the egg mixture: In a bowl, whisk together 4 large eggs, 1 cup Pecorino Romano cheese, grated, 0.5 cup Parmesan cheese, grated, and 2 teaspoons freshly cracked black pepper until smooth.
  4. Combine pasta and guanciale: Remove the skillet from heat and add the drained spaghetti directly to the pan with the guanciale, tossing to coat in the rendered fat.
  5. Emulsify the sauce: Off the heat, pour the egg mixture over the pasta, tossing quickly and continuously. Add splashes of reserved pasta water as needed until the sauce turns glossy and coats the noodles.
  6. Finish and serve: Plate immediately and top with extra Pecorino Romano cheese, grated and a generous crack of black pepper. Serve hot.
