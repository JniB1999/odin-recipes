Thai Green Curry

← Home Page

A vibrant, aromatic curry built on a paste of green chilies, lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaf, garlic, and shallots, simmered with rich coconut milk until silky and fragrant. Tender chicken (or your choice of protein) mingles with Thai eggplant, bamboo shoots, and bell peppers, all soaking up the curry's signature balance of heat, sweetness, and salt. Finished with a scattering of fresh Thai basil and a squeeze of lime, it's served steaming hot over jasmine rice — comforting, complex, and unmistakably Thai.
