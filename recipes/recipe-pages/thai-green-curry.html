Thai Green Curry

← Home Page

A vibrant & aromatic curry built on a paste of green chilies, lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaf, garlic, and shallots, simmered with rich coconut milk until silky and fragrant. Tender chicken (or your choice of protein) mingles with Thai eggplant, bamboo shoots, and bell peppers, all soaking up the curry's signature balance of heat, sweetness, and salt. Finished with a scattering of fresh Thai basil and a squeeze of lime, it's served steaming hot over jasmine rice — comforting, complex, and unmistakably Thai.

Ingrediant sizing is intended for 4 servings:

  • 3 tablespoons green curry paste
  • 2 cups of coconut milk, divided
  • 1 pound of boneless chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoons palm suagr - or brown sugar
  • 1 cups Thai eggplants, quartered
  • 1 cups red bell pepper, sliced
  • 0.5 cups bamboo shoots, sliced
  • 3 kaffir lime leaves, torn
  • 1 cups fresh Thai basil leaves
  • 1 red chilli, sliced

Steps:

  1. Fry the paste: Heat 1 tablespoons vegetable oil in a pot over medium heat. Add 3 tablespoons green curry paste and fry for 2 minutes 02:00 until fragrant.
  2. Add coconut cream: Add the thick part of 2 cups coconut milk, divided (about half a cup) and stir until the oil separates and it's fragrant, about 4 minutes
